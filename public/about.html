$UPIC (BETA)
Instrument X

UPIC is a live audiovisual instrument for cryptocurrency markets.

Named after Iannis Xenakis’s pioneering UPIC system—which transformed drawings into synthesized sound—the project extends the relationship between data, notation, image and music into contemporary internet-native markets.

Drawing from Xenakis, John Cage’s experiments with graphic notation, and Ryoji Ikeda’s audiovisual treatment of data, UPIC approaches market information as compositional material.

Price, volatility, volume, liquidity and activity are translated into rhythm, pitch, density, texture, movement and form.

Each token produces a continuously evolving audiovisual identity shaped by the behavior of its market.

Rather than visualizing a chart or indicating what to trade, UPIC makes the market perceptible as an instrument: something that can be watched, heard, performed, recorded and shared.
