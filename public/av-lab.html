What this lab covers

Actual sampled EarthBound harmony and arpeggios, ENVION’s market-mapped controls, five seeded Pure Data market phrases, data sonification, and optional AI arpeggios. Tempo is limited to the existing melodic engine’s 40–140 BPM range.

It simulates market inputs; it does not fetch trades or reproduce a historical recording. The arpeggio model downloads only when you request it. ENVION sample replacement, internal Pd patch wiring, model weights and convolution impulse lengths are not editable here. Exported notes and presets do not change the main website.

04 / VISUAL CONTROLSContext, marks, motion

Both outputs share the same market and audio clock. Marks are circles at neutral price change, + for gains and × for losses. AUTO Pixel survival decays more slowly at higher market caps. A cable controls its destination; otherwise AUTO or the manual value applies.

Audio level is measured before listening volume. Marks dissolve after sound stops. Market inputs are simulated. EarthBound presets adapt motion, not original game artwork.
