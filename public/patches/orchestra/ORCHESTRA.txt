AV / MARKET INSTRUMENT

Active sources: original Envion 5.2,
and an independently authored hardstyle kick inspired by GrundTon's demo:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v5btDT3sjao
The kick uses pitch decay and successive filtering/distortion stages; it is
an approximation, not the original patch or recorded video audio.

Hardstyle is eligible when available market cap is >= $1,000,000.
Every four bars, market activity/pressure gives a 15–45% chance of a short
four-beat phrase, followed by at least twelve beats of rest.
Below the threshold, unavailable cap or stale signals close its trigger gate.
History playback uses the chart's estimated historical capitalization.
Market cap tempo: $10k=10 BPM, $1m=100 BPM, $10m=200 BPM.

Harmonic strings are OFF by default at the user's request. They can be restored
explicitly in pdata. They use sustained partials, slow detuning and stereo delay.
Market data controls register, brightness and level. This is NOT CoDiCodec
inference. The referenced custom violin checkpoint and samples were unavailable:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DqQvzOBhg8E
https://github.com/moiseshorta/CoDiCodec-Flow

Type pdata into the address box to remove/restore each complete sound bundle.
Pause and listening volume apply to the entire stereo mix.
Gameta, ZERO100 and Perc Generator are removed from the published engine.

Rebuild: python3 build-strings.py; python3 build-celebration.py;
python3 build-orchestra.py. Tests: node checks/market-layers.mjs.
Envion provenance: ../envion/PORT-NOTES.txt and ../envion/LICENSE.txt.
