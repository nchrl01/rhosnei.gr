AV / AUTO ORCHESTRA

Browser: enter a contract, choose its indexed pool and press Listen.
There is one ensemble. The five meters display automatic part gain, not selectors.
All parts share a sixteenth-note clock and the pitch family 0 2 3 5 7 9 10.
Every 32 ticks (two bars) phrase roles change. Activity and USD volume set a
bounded total gain budget; movement and liquidity distribute prominence.
Mix changes use a 650 ms time constant. Missing snapshot-only signals fade.
Master adjusts listening volume only. This is a rule-based conductor, not AI.

Parts: Envion 8 stereo live-fragment gestures; ZERO100 32 tonal lanes / 12 polyrhythms /
8 resonant bands; Perc Generator 32 synthesized percussion voices.
The percussion uses FM and filtered noise, not sampled breaks or ZENOLOGY.
ZENOLOGY remains a separately hosted local plugin companion.

Desktop: keep this folder together, open av-desktop.pd, enable DSP, start the
local AV server, choose Native Pd on localhost:4173, then Listen.
The bridge validates the orchestra version and stops after missing heartbeats.
Do not open another market instrument alongside it: controls are global.

Reference adaptations:
Rolando Rampoldi / Gameta https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Atttrb0hoEc
ZeroPoint Zero / 100h https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ziJ4md4IvKY
ZeroPoint Zero / Perc Generator https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CpNo7f6aMnI
Original hidden Gen code was unavailable. DSP and transitions are independent
approximations, not original patch ports or verified identical sound.
GrundTon ambient breakcore https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZMCVewUXCY remains
an aesthetic reference; its original patch and drum samples are not bundled.
No reference video audio, commercial plugin, or preset is distributed.

Rebuild: python3 build-orchestra.py (in the project root).
Runtime check: node checks/orchestra.mjs uses the actual embedded browser WASM.
Voice pulses report actual Pd triggers/envelopes. L/R meters measure output.
Native telemetry is sampled and cannot show every note event.

Envion: Emiliano Pennisi (2025), MIT. See ENVION-LICENSE.txt.
https://www.peamarte.it/env/envion_v3.6.html
The value/time/delay triplet core is adapted to vanilla Pd; the full PlugData
application, its externals, presets, samples and network loader are not bundled.
AV reshapes its own live tonal/polyrhythmic audio with gesture envelopes.
The live six-second buffer fills after playback starts.

Market context: latest received state + fixed five-minute candles, independently
of chart zoom/pan. Return size/speed and recent shocks retain recent intensity;
shocks cool with a two-hour time constant. Volume relative to loaded history and
liquidity turnover also contribute. Missing context remains explicit. Typical
market cap is a price-based estimate using snapshot supply, not historical cap.
