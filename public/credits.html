The current instrument adapts Rolando Rampoldi’s Gameta: rule-changing cellular automata (Creative Commons Attribution video). The visible genotype/phenotype expressions are reconstructed in native Pd, with AV market mappings and independently implemented strings, pads and stereo space. See reference, changes and attribution. Related library: PDida. No video audio or PDida source is bundled.

The automatic ensemble also adapts the visible structure of ZeroPoint Zero’s 100h and Perc Generator. Hidden Gen algorithms are independent approximations; original source and video audio are not included. Orchestra notes. GrundTon’s ambient breakcore remains an aesthetic reference; original drum samples are not included.
