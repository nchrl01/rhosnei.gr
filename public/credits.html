Seeded math functions

The library contains 13 equations transcribed from the user-supplied @indent126 reel and the ten named functions from the @the.lab67 reel. Five distinct functions are selected per coin and unlock at $100K, $500K, $1M, $2M and $5M. They play finite, tempo-synchronized phrases in the existing vanilla Pure Data voices. Cartesian plots progressively draw the literal equations at the same phrase position as the bounded audio controls. The first reel supplies equations without names, so those titles are descriptive. The second reel's headings are preserved.

The displayed mathematical domains are inferred from unticked source axes. Pitch range, timbre and amplitude are authored, bounded sonification choices; original reel audio is not bundled. Data microtones

The new set uses original vanilla Pure Data sources: five finite seeded voices—high ticks, a longer sine pulse, filtered noise, middle pulses and a low sine cluster. Rhythm follows the market tempo and measured activity; movement shapes pitch, timbre and filtering; observed buy/sell balance shapes stereo position. Synthesis principles were studied in Sound Simulator’s tutorial and its creator-provided patch. Its source or recordings are not bundled as an Ikeda work. This is an authored market adaptation.

Trade piano and arpeggios

The trade piano uses CC0 VSCO 2 CE upright-piano recordings by Simon Dalzell / Ivy Audio, distributed by Versilian Studios. License · Sample provenance. Piano plays individual harmonic notes at 5% cumulative price movement since its previous note. An initial contextual price anchors the first move. The light sample pack keeps eight notes in 16 kHz mono PCM16, trimmed to six seconds and normalized offline. Fewer overlapping voices, complete release ramps and a fixed stereo room preserve bounded playback workload. The piano retains its clear sampled attack with longer releases and a stronger, memory-capped eight-second diffuse reverb; cap controls warmth and the wet/dry balance. Harmonic stages advance with accepted notes, with nearest-tone movement and deterministic timing/touch variations. Connected decoded feeds can allow quiet single notes after 30 seconds without an observed trade, then at coin-seeded 45–60-second intervals. Replay applies movement selection to completed candles and allows quiet notes only during zero-volume candles; its quiet spacing uses listening time. Arpeggio phrases are eligible only after significant moves. Harmonic strings, Gameta, ZERO100 and Perc Generator are removed from the active engine. The standalone hardstyle instrument is removed from the active engine. No video audio or CoDiCodec model is bundled in the website.

Open source musical AI and voice

Occasional arpeggios use Magenta MusicRNN chord-pitches improv. Neural contour is generated once per coin using neural probabilities and a coin-seeded draw, then projected onto the current piano chord. A seeded phrase plays while the model loads or when it is unavailable. Shared scores freeze that phrase. Magenta license · TensorFlow license.

Phrasing references: Tone.js humanization and Magenta expressive timing and dynamics. These inform authored phrasing; no Performance RNN model or Tone.js runtime is loaded. Coin names use Kokoro 82M, quantized ONNX, female voice af_nicole at 0.78 speaking speed, followed by an authored noise-vocoder whisper effect that reduces pitched excitation and preserves a little articulation. This is a processed voice, not a whisper-trained model. Inference runs in a worker on the listener’s device; model and voice files download from Hugging Face and the matching ONNX runtime from jsDelivr. Voice passes through a warm 4.8-second stereo hall with 32 ms predelay and a filtered reverb return. Announcements require playback, observed trading or price movement and sustained audible output from the other instruments. Low volatility alone does not block the voice. Interrupted phrases can retry after four seconds; only completed phrases enter the long cooldown. A dedicated instrument tap excludes the voice and its own reverb. Announcements stop when that instrument signal becomes silent, have a bounded duration, and share the master volume and audio capture. No browser speech voice is substituted. Interface references

$UPIC’s header mark is the user-supplied image, dithered with the same pixel engine. The live/transport lamp follows the compact dot treatment on NTS, with explicit live, delayed, historical and paused states.

Full-image ordered Bayer dithering inspired by Tooooools. Readable image pixels use the original 8×8 charcoal dot pattern; hosts that block pixel access use a source-only SVG stochastic dither. There is no edge fade. The browser signal field reconstructs the horizontal-row workflow credited below. Measured final stereo sound gates its visibility: silence, mute, pause and seeking clear the surface. Activity, volume, price direction and liquidity shape its density, travel and grain while audible sound and reverb keep it moving. Replay follows the advancing chart cursor. Desktop fills its assigned right-hand area; mobile has a separate opaque panel below the player with no overlay or labels. The page-wide NDS/LCD mask has been removed.

The light floating-label field and price-history tick navigator are independent implementations inspired by Bencho’s label input and progress ticks. The tick readouts and heights use AV’s loaded market data.
