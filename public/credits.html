Credits & source

The current instrument adapts Rolando Rampoldi’s Gameta: rule-changing cellular automata (Creative Commons Attribution video). The visible genotype/phenotype expressions are reconstructed in native Pd, with AV market mappings and independently implemented pads and stereo space; the earlier string bank remains in the archived standalone study. See reference, changes and attribution. Related library: PDida. No video audio or PDida source is bundled.

The automatic ensemble also adapts the visible structure of ZeroPoint Zero’s 100h and Perc Generator. Hidden Gen algorithms are independent approximations; original source and video audio are not included. Orchestra notes. GrundTon’s ambient breakcore remains an aesthetic reference; original drum samples are not included.

Envion gesture part: adapted from Envion by Emiliano Pennisi (2025). Its value/time/delay triplet core shapes fragments of AV’s own live audio. This is a portable vanilla-Pd adaptation, not the full PlugData application. No Envion audio samples or network loader are bundled. MIT license and attribution.

Dynamic market sketch: an independent Canvas/Web Audio implementation inspired by JeongHo Park’s SonicSketch path/spectrum approach. Fixed market history and the latest observation supply the path; AV’s actual Pd events and measured audio supply motion and texture. SonicSketch code, assets and synthesizer are not bundled.

Wersi cartridge reader: independently implemented from the documented MK1 block layout, cross-referenced with DMS-Toolbox by Michael Kukat. The website contains no ROM wave data. Local cartridge waves are played with Web Audio; envelope, filter, register selection and tuning are approximations.

Pure Data browser runtime: hyrfilm/libpd-wasm. Distributed with its license notice.

Market snapshots: DEX Screener API. Availability depends on indexed pools and provider access.
