Envion 5.2

The instrument now runs the supplied original Envion by Emiliano Pennisi (© 2025, MIT), with its DSP, presets, effects and full supplied sample library. Its source model contains 109 canvases, 5,597 nodes and 4,380 wires; the library contains 44 audio files, 19 envelope banks and six tape impulse responses. Original license and notices.

AV adds browser controls, keyboard and file handling, and routes the original stereo output through the market-driven ensemble, liquidity-controlled Freeverb and master volume. Original patch recording controls use a browser WAV writer fed by the actual Envion mix before its output stage; other unbound source inputs remain silent. This recording is separate from AV’s main post-master recording. The original four-triplet envelope path and 44,100 Hz timing are retained. Port notes, source checksum and desktop setup.

This is an original-source port with host adaptations. Sample waveforms are shown; scopes without a live signal tap are labelled “No live tap,” and changing canvas labels are not mirrored. NETaudio depends on browser access and remote availability; compressed audio may be resampled by the browser decoder. PCM WAV source data is preserved.

Original Envion patch · Full project and sample library. For desktop use, copy the original patch and its audio, data and asset folders into a separate folder, then use PlugData with the original libraries and search paths described in the port notes. AV’s Native Pd selector is temporarily disabled because the full browser file bridge is not implemented by the desktop host; older orchestra archives are version-4 studies.

Other musical references

The instrument also adapts Rolando Rampoldi’s Gameta: rule-changing cellular automata (Creative Commons Attribution video). The visible genotype/phenotype expressions are reconstructed in Pd, with AV market mappings and independently implemented stereo space; earlier strings and pads remain in the archived standalone study. See reference, changes and attribution. Related library: PDida. No video audio or PDida source is bundled.

The automatic ensemble also adapts the visible structure of ZeroPoint Zero’s 100h and Perc Generator. Their hidden Gen algorithms remain independent approximations, not verified identical recreations; original source and video audio are not included. Orchestra notes. GrundTon’s ambient breakcore remains an aesthetic reference; original drum samples are not included.

Dynamic market sketch: an independent Canvas/Web Audio implementation inspired by JeongHo Park’s SonicSketch path/spectrum approach. Fixed market history and the latest observation supply the path; AV’s actual Pd events and measured audio supply motion and texture. SonicSketch code, assets and synthesizer are not bundled.

Freeverb: vanilla-Pd adaptation of the public-domain algorithm by Jezar at Dreampoint (June 2000). Original source; port and tuning notes.
