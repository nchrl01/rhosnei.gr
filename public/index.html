Chain coverage & data sources

Token discovery and snapshots work across chains indexed by DEX Screener, including non-EVM token identifiers. Search results can be limited by the provider; select the network and pool explicitly.

Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Polygon, Optimism and Avalanche have free PublicNode adapters for standard V2/V3 pool swaps. EVM events are observed after block inclusion and may be reversed by a chain reorganization. Solana streams confirmed pool transactions; these are not decoded swaps.

GeckoTerminal supplies a free five-second trade-polling fallback across its indexed networks and pools. Its first response establishes a baseline; existing trades are not replayed as new events. Unindexed pools, different network identifiers, unsupported payloads and unavailable feeds retain snapshots. Polling and streams have different delays, shown in the source labels.

The chart and music consume the same received trades. Candles summarize observations during this session, not complete exchange OHLC history. Native streamed USD prices use the latest snapshot quote conversion; polled trade USD values come from the provider. Snapshot and trade polling run every five seconds; upstream caching may delay updates. Public endpoints can throttle traffic. Disconnects and fallback are shown above.

Layer levels follow market signals automatically. Listening volume adjusts playback only. Stream gaps are not backfilled; stale snapshot-driven layers fade. Demo signals are synthetic.
