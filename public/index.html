Chain coverage & data sources

Token discovery and snapshots work across chains indexed by DEX Screener, including non-EVM token identifiers. Search results can be limited by the provider; select the network and pool explicitly.

Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Polygon, Optimism and Avalanche have free PublicNode adapters for standard V2/V3 pool swaps. Robinhood Chain Uniswap v4 pools have a direct public RPC adapter with polling at intervals of at least two seconds. Other v4 deployments remain unsupported. EVM events are observed after block inclusion and may be reversed by a chain reorganization. Solana Orca Whirlpools decode confirmed swap events directly. Other Solana pools stream transaction activity without decoding swaps.

GeckoTerminal supplies a free cached trade-polling fallback across its indexed networks and pools. Its first response establishes a baseline; existing trades are not replayed as new events. Unindexed pools, different network identifiers, unsupported payloads and unavailable feeds retain snapshots. Polling runs at intervals of at least eight seconds and shares a request budget with history. The free provider documents a one-minute cache. Polling and streams have different delays, shown in the source labels.

The chart and music consume the same received trades. Origin context loads available provider OHLC history for the selected pool; live candles summarize received observations. First known market creation and earliest available prices are shown separately. Neither establishes a verified token launch date. Native streamed USD prices use the latest snapshot quote conversion; polled trade USD values come from the provider. Snapshots poll every five seconds. Trade polling is slower and provider caching may delay updates. Public endpoints can throttle traffic. Disconnects and fallback are shown above.
