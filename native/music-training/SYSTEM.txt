UPIC arrangement engine — release 209

Market observations -> mood group, harmonic character, intensity, tempo.
Coin seed -> stable key, melodic voice, motif identity, Kraken bass voice.
All roles share a beat timeline and the same chord at a bar boundary.

Below $10k: 0.70 melodic reverb send, 0.30 direct level.
From $10k to $10m: logarithmic increase in directness, decrease in send.
Above $1m: offbeat chord stabs, sixteenth-note syncopation, octave bass figures.
These changes do not add drums.

Two public-domain Bach MIDI editions train a second-order interval-count
model. It suggests melodic movement. Chord tones own accented beats;
diatonic connectors may fill weak beats. Eight-bar call/answer variation
and cadences are composed rules. This is not a large neural model.
The optional existing MusicRNN receives the chart's chord progression;
its output is revoiced to the coin's current key and frozen for a replay.

GeneralUser 2.0.3 stereo multisamples: four key centres, two velocities.
FluidSynth renders its programmed timbre offline; the browser only loads
the selected bank. Kraken 1/2 remain EarthBound bass samples.

Replay integrates tempo across frozen candle observations. Seeking restores
musical phase and sustained sample position instead of invoking a new phrase.
The renderer's motion phase likewise follows source time. This is a score
transport, not a decoded video/audio recording: reverb history and the
separate granular Envion patch are not sample-exact across arbitrary seeks.
Use an audio recording when bit-identical playback of the whole mix matters.

Data Pd voices receive observation bangs only. The master metronome no longer
feeds their trigger. Density changes event probability, not autonomous timing.
Reverb send remains at 0.65–0.80 and its feedback decay is longer.
