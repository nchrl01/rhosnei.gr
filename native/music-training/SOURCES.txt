UPIC compact interval model training sources

J. S. Bach: Inventions 10 (BWV 781) and 11 (BWV 782).
MIDI editions typeset by Allen Garvin, Mutopia Project.
Both source LilyPond headers explicitly state "Public Domain" and the
public-domain dedication by the typesetter. Retrieved 2026-10-07.

https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BachJS/BWV781/bach-invention-10/
https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BachJS/BWV782/bach-invention-11/

The browser uses aggregate second-order interval counts, not a recording
or direct playback of these pieces. Rhythm, form, harmony, instruments and
market mappings are UPIC's composition rules. This small model is not a
large neural music model and is not a claim of musical intelligence.

Rebuild: python3 scripts/train-motif-model.py
Additional licensed MIDI inputs can be passed as arguments. Keep each
voice monophonic for meaningful interval transitions.
