Every tone, drum, packet and noise comes from two coordinated GB chips. Drag knobs, use arrow keys, or type a value. AUTO follows the market; turn a knob to override it. Changes stay in this lab. Start audio is required before anything plays.

What this lab covers

Two Game Boy chips provide lead, arps, bass, held chords, drone and grain-like noise. Shared phrases and pauses coordinate their eight channels. Market observations shape recurring motifs, harmony, grain spacing, drum intensity and articulation. Scored echoes and wave resonance add depth inside the chips.

This lab simulates market inputs; it does not fetch trades or reproduce a historical recording. Chip controls change duty cycles, stepped envelopes, noise clocks, wave tables and stereo routing. Sound is generated within the chip, with no external sample layers or reverb. Presets store controls and chosen drum variations.
