GeneralUser GS 2.0.3 by S. Christian Collins
https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS
https://schristiancollins.com/generaluser.php

Selected presets rendered offline through FluidSynth 2.4.6 using
js-synthesizer 1.13.0. Stereo WAV, 32 kHz, two velocity layers,
four key centres, normalized with crossfaded sustain loops.
The bank's programmed filters, modulation and stereo layers are baked in.
UPIC applies its own scored note envelopes and market-controlled reverb.
These are sampled approximations of the presets, not the full live SF2 synth.

Build: scripts/render-generaluser.cjs <js-synthesizer package> <GeneralUser-GS.sf2>
The rendering dependency is only needed to rebuild assets, not to run UPIC.
